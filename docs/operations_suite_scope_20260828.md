# Scope of this PR

This PR implements the P0 architecture foundation and exposes the target user experience while reusing stable business engines. It is deliberately incremental rather than a greenfield rewrite.

Included: portal, five workspaces, shared design system, canonical Site/Warehouse masters, LBA funding-cycle/limit foundations, central Bag Management approvals and multi-release engine, Traceability 360 projection/search, Supabase-driven XLSX export, RLS/index hardening for new objects.

Not included: historical Excel transaction import, unconfirmed Finance formulas/thresholds, full rewrite of every legacy RCN screen, expanded post-calibration Factory process, or a real-world field pilot.
